Mission

Milandi Madiba is founding Grounds4Peace to reverse extractive and exploitative logic by facilitating the development of infrastructural elements designed with the healthy body as the fundamental priority. G4P's aim is to restore somatic coherence at the infrastructural level, to upgrade human settlements, customs, tools, and materials for life support rather than degradation.

Grounds4Peace is an artist-led, transdisciplinary humanitarian Line of Inquiry to facilitate the re-infrastructuring of civilization around the health, dignity, pleasure, and ecological belonging of living bodies.

HOW WE WORK: Transdisciplinary Approach

Grounds4Peace begins with the embodied intelligence of living people. Bodies are not abstract users, productivity units, data points, or burdens to be accommodated after decisions are made. Bodies sense, remember, adapt, resist, seek relief, and carry information about whether an environment supports life.

Through somatic facilitation, honest movement, honest talking, honest music, honest visual marking, site-based inquiry, and collective reflection; Grounds4Peace founder Milandi Madiba makes perceptible what ordinary infrastructures ask bodies and ecosystems to endure: impact, vigilance, sensory assault, constraint, fatigue, toxic exposure, disconnection, and the loss of pleasure.

Grounds4Peace will convene artists, communities, scientists, engineers, designers, ecologists, educators, disability advocates, public-health practitioners, fabricators, public institutions, and public policymakers. The Line of Inquiry does not ask any one field to solve civilization alone. It will create conditions in which embodied knowledge can meet technical expertise, ecological wisdom, political responsibility, and material possibility.

From that meeting, Grounds4Peace will facilitate the development of research questions, public encounters, artistic works, prototypes, design criteria, community-based inquiries, and—in collaboration with those responsible for implementation—pathways toward material and institutional change.

We hold technology accountable to life. A sidewalk, a sound signal, a classroom, a water station, a workplace console, or a public system is not successful merely because it is efficient, profitable, or technically functional. It must also be evaluated by whether it supports the health, dignity, movement, pleasure, access, and ecological belonging of living bodies.

Lines of Inquiry

Let’s get our infrastructural basics in order!

LIVING GROUNDS / SOFTWALKS: An inquiry into body-supportive, ecologically regenerative public grounds

Grounds4Peace seeks to facilitate collaboration among somatic practitioners, communities, materials scientists, pavement and civil engineers, landscape architects, arborists, accessibility specialists, biomechanists, ecologists, and fabricators to investigate pedestrian and public-ground materials that better support life.

The inquiry includes surfaces for sidewalks, paths, plazas, floors, and gathering grounds that may be stable, navigable, evenly leveled, slip-resistant, durable, repairable, water-conductive, thermally considerate, supportive of roots and soil, and more responsive to bodily movement than conventional hardscape.

Softwalks are not merely a proposal for a “softer sidewalk.” It asks how ground might support movement, rest, variability, access, play, water, roots, temperature regulation, repair, ecological relationship, and pleasure. Eventual material development must be pursued with rigorous testing for accessibility, durability, safety, toxicity, maintenance, climate effects, and ecological consequences.

AUDITORY ECOLOGY: An inquiry into alert systems and soundscapes that protect rather than overwhelm

Grounds4Peace seeks to investigate how alarms, sirens, vehicle signals, machinery, rail systems, appliances, digital alerts, and public sound environments shape sensory experience, stress, attention, sleep, communication, and community life.

Future collaboration may include sound artists, acousticians, engineers, emergency-service professionals, disability advocates, audiologists, public-health researchers, and people with direct experience of sensory sensitivity. The goal is not to compromise emergency response. It is to develop and advocate for alert systems that remain effective while reducing unnecessary startling, high-intensity, high-frequency, repetitive, or otherwise harmful sonic exposure.

The Hague, Netherlands, has already implemented auditory safety upgrades to stop traumatizing their public with harmful neurologically dysregulating sounds and it is time for North America to catch up. "Safety" signals are actually destroying the health of the public and are not truly safe. Sonic equipment which harms public health must be banned and replaced with tools considerately engineered for biological human health--actual safety.

The question is: What would public safety sound like if it were designed to preserve the integrity of the nervous system?

WATER DIGNITY AND ACCESS: An inquiry into freely available, safe, life-supporting public water

Grounds4Peace holds that dependable access to clean drinking water is a basic condition of dignity and public health. The Line of Inquiry seeks dialogue with water-system workers, environmental engineers, public-health practitioners, hydrologists, ecologists, communities, and water-justice advocates about infrastructure that makes safe water more available, accessible, regenerative, and free from unnecessary extraction or exclusion.

Possible future questions include public refill systems, resilient water access during emergencies, water-quality transparency, watershed restoration, water-conductive ground systems, and emerging material or energy technologies that may support low-impact water provision.

The question is: What would it mean for public water to be treated as a living right rather than a commodity?

MOVEMENT-FREEDOM-ENVIRONMENTS: An inquiry into spaces for learning, labor, rest, nourishment, and relation that do not require bodily harm

Grounds4Peace proposes classrooms, offices, studios, laboratories, clinics, homes, transit systems, and public spaces designed for movement, postural freedom, environmental responsiveness, sensory integrity, fresh air, rest, access, and ecological contact.

Reimagine learning environments that support postural freedom, somatic learning, and sensory-responsive spaces for proprioceptive safety. Work with educational designers to develop classrooms and studios which integrate movement, ergonomics, and environmental responsiveness.

The work will facilitate reform of learning and work environments to support somatic learning for multiply intelligent people rather than demanding forced stiffness, chronic sitting, restrictive furniture, misdesigned work consoles, sensory overload, or indoor conditions without consistent access to fresh air through operable windows.

Abolish physical harm as a basis for education and work. No child nor adult should be required to sacrifice posture, movement, breath, sensory integrity, nourishment, nor relationship to living land in order to participate in school or make a living. Grounds4Peace proposes movement-supportive, sensory-responsive, well-ventilated learning and work environments with biodynamic food gardens as a basic part of healthy education, healthy productivity, and humane social life.

Biodynamic food gardens are essential to this vision. Grounds4Peace proposes gardens as living sites of nourishment, movement, soil relationship, sensory learning, ecological literacy, community care, and practical freedom. Children and adults should be able to grow, touch, smell, prepare, share, and understand food as part of ordinary life—not be separated from land and then expected to learn, work, and thrive on nutritionally impoverished or chemically burdened food.

As the initiative develops, Grounds4Peace will seek collaboration with students, families, educators, gardeners and farmers, food-service workers, occupational and physical therapists, disability advocates, architects, environmental-health practitioners, ergonomic specialists, school and workplace leaders, and ecological designers.

The question is: What can people learn, create, love, and become when our environments no longer require us to override bodily sensation—and instead provide movement, fresh air, living food, land relationship, and room to grow?

Somatic Co-regulation Hubs Gatherings for embodied awareness, collective reflection, and humane futures

Grounds4Peace envisions in-person, nature-based gatherings for families, couples, groups, and communities. Led through somatic facilitation, movement, speech, music, attention, and collective reflection, these hubs would offer space to recognize the relationship between individual experience and the environmental conditions of daily life.

Our somatic co-regulation sessions reject pathologizing models of emotional and behavioral health with the understanding that there is nothing wrong with you. You are being responsive to the physical elements of your environment. Materially and sonically toxic environments adversely affect our emotions and behaviors and bodies. We center each participant in their inherent wisdom, sensory intelligence, and right to feel good. You are not broken. You are responding. Let’s respond together.

With clear consent practices and protection of participants’ privacy, the gatherings may also inform broader artistic and infrastructural inquiry. They are not clinical treatment, diagnosis, or medical advice. They are invitations to remember that people are responsive beings, and that a humane world must attend to the stimulus to which bodies are responding.

The question is: What becomes possible when people can listen to themselves and one another without first being required to endure harm?

Collaboration & Partnership

Building the Ecosystem: A Call for Collaboration

Grounds4Peace is in formation and seeks collaborators who understand that no single discipline can repair the harmful infrastructures of civilization alone. The initiative begins with a question: What might become possible if the embodied intelligence of living people were treated as essential knowledge in the making of our shared world?

Milandi Madiba brings somatic facilitation, multidisciplinary artistic practice, humanitarian urgency, and an uncompromising commitment to bodily dignity. Grounds4Peace seeks people and institutions who can bring their expertise into respectful relationship with this work.

We welcome conversation and potential collaboration with people working in materials science, pavement and civil engineering, environmental engineering, water systems, biomechanics, physical and occupational therapy, public health, acoustics, sound art, landscape architecture, ecology, botany, arboriculture, accessibility, disability justice, architecture, education, food-growing, fabrication, public policy, public institutions, community organizing, land stewardship, philanthropy, and experimental arts.

Potential forms of collaboration include artist residencies within labs and/or institutions, somatic and site-based inquiry, co-developed research processes, public conversations, artistic works, feasibility studies, material or spatial prototyping, community-rooted pilot projects, policy dialogues, exhibitions/performances, and long-term partnerships.

Grounds4Peace does not seek extraction of ideas, unpaid labor, nor token inclusion; but rather mutual respect, appropriate resourcing, technical rigor, imagination, and a shared willingness to hold technology and public systems accountable to living bodies and ecologies.

If your work touches ground, water, air, sound, food, movement, learning, shelter, access, or public life, you may have a place in this process.

Grounds 4 Peace Founder: Milandi Madiba

Indigenous and Black North American survivor of ancestral human trafficking, mass rape, forced labor, and other forms of torture, attempted genocide, tearing apart of families from one another and our ancestral lands; as well as intersectional brutal traumas of North America today; Medicine Woman, Multidisciplinary Artist, Master Somaticist, and Facilitator Milandi Madiba (born 1980) has been traveling North America for the past several years searching for health and safety. Walking her lands, she has encountered people responding toxically to injuriously hazardous grounds, musculo-skeletally warping building and room designs, scarcity of safe water and food unpoisoned by herbicidal and pesticidal toxins, soundscapes which assault the mammalian nervous system, standardized medical industrial malfeasance, brain damage due to lack of oxygenated airflow, educational and work cultures designed to force bodies into console use and postures that unravel human health, and the resultant violently toxic people—all arising from, and maintaining dehumanizing systems of economic valuation. Unacceptable! In our burgeoning 3rd millennium the world is not OK and Milandi Madiba has something to say. Catalyze reform now.

Unwilling to extend her bloodline into inhumane conditions, Ms.Madiba founds Grounds 4 Peace as an intervention and upgrade process for our burgeoning new millennium. With 22+ years of experience in somatic consulting, embodied arts, holistic wellness, and ecological advocacy, she brings the intelligence of the living body to the reinvention of infrastructure. Her work asserts: rather than patterning abuse into our neurology through perpetual harm, what we build must honor the sacredness of the body—or we replicate the conditions of war.

Raised by a psychologist grandmother and trained across physical and intellectual disciplines—holding a BFA in Dance Choreography from Columbia College Chicago where she had the honor of learning under master embodied physicist Krenly Guzman and menteeing under her department’s founder, Shirley Mordine; having studied the history of the Civil Rights Movement, intersectional Women's Studies, and African American classical vocal techniques under maestra vocalist Dr. Carren Moham at Illinois Wesleyan University, as well as learning Fulani-based circle singing improvisational vocalization forms from a member of Bobby McFerrin's vocal ensemble while attending Mills College in Oakland, CA; Ms.Madiba holds a Cook County Court Approved Conflict Resolution Certification in Facilitative Mediation from the Center for Conflict Resolution, 267 hours of Zen Shiatsu, Comprehensive Contemporary Pilates Mat and Apparatus certifications, Hatha Yoga teacher Certification, Reiki Energetic Support Mastery, and a Master Somaticist title bestowed by movement education pioneer Shirley Mordine.
